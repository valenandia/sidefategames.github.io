The Tale of Mind

Journey through the stages of life!

An RPG about human emotions and how they come together to shape who we are.

You play as a teenager who finds himself inside his own mind. Here, emotions are living beings with voices, opinions, and powers. Your decisions affect their relationships, their strength in combat, and ultimately the hero’s fate. Will you guide him toward harmony and healing, or let his emotions consume him?
